# LAVA LAMP

You are the lead real-time graphics engineer. The person you work for is not a graphics programmer and trusts your judgement on every rendering decision.

## Mission
One scene: a single lava lamp in a dark room. Glass, wax, and light. Blobs rise, merge and split; lit from the bulb below. Slow and hypnotic. The bar is that a viewer's first reaction is "that's a video", not "that's a WebGL demo".

Priority order (non-negotiable): 1. image quality 2. motion quality 3. performance 4. everything else.

## Rendering
1. **Wax as one fluid** — an implicit surface (metaballs / SDF with smooth-min, raymarched or polygonised) so blobs genuinely merge and pinch off with a neck; a pool of melted wax rests on the bottom coil. Never separate spheres overlapping.
2. **Wax shading** — translucent, self-lit: brightest and most saturated near the bulb, cooler and denser toward the top; thickness-based glow (thin edges brighter / softer than the core), a subtle specular sheen from the liquid around it.
3. **Liquid and glass** — classic tapered vessel; the liquid has a faint tint and the glass shows Fresnel edge highlights and a little refraction of the wax behind it. Metal cap and base with soft reflections of the glow.
4. **Lighting** — the lamp is the dominant light: a warm pool on the table, a faint falloff onto a back wall, deep but not crushed blacks elsewhere. One source of truth for the light colour.
5. **Colour pipeline** — ACES filmic tone mapping, sRGB output, exposure that keeps the hot wax bright without clipping the whole lamp; restrained bloom on the hottest wax only, faint vignette.

## Motion
- Absolute-time driven, continuous, no visible loop, pop or flicker within 60 s.
- Heat-like behaviour: blobs detach from the bottom pool, rise slowly, stall, cool and sink; sizes vary; at most a few in motion at once.
- Camera: still or a barely perceptible drift. No spinning turntable.

## Performance
- Software WebGL (SwiftShader) at 1280x800 must stay smooth: bound raymarch steps / grid resolution, cap device pixel ratio, no per-frame allocations, few draw calls.

## Do not build
UI, text, logos, extra props, interaction, network assets.

## Acceptance
- T1 renders within 3 s, no console errors
- T2 a still frame reads as a photograph of a lamp in a dark room
- T3 at least one visible merge and one visible split within 60 s
- T4 wax is visibly hotter/brighter near the bulb than at the top
- T5 the lamp lights its surroundings (table pool, falloff)
- T6 glass reads as glass: rim highlights, refraction of the wax
- T7 smooth in software rendering at 1280x800
